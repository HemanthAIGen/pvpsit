About PVPSIT

Institution profile, recognition and campus overview

Prasad V. Potluri Siddhartha Institute of Technology

Established in 1998, PVPSIT is a self-financed autonomous institution sponsored by Siddhartha Academy of General and Technical Education. The campus is located at Kanuru, Vijayawada.

  • Autonomous status since 2012
  • Approved by AICTE; permanently affiliated to JNTUK, Kakinada
  • NAAC A+ accredited; undergraduate programmes accredited by NBA
  • ISO 21001:2018 certification as stated on the current college website
  • UGC 2(f)/12(B) status as stated in the institutional profile

Admissions

Undergraduate and postgraduate programmes

Undergraduate admissions

B.Tech programmes include CSE, CSE (AI & ML), CSE (Data Science), IT, ECE, EEE, Mechanical and Civil Engineering. Admissions and eligibility are subject to the current AP EAPCET / convenor-quota and management-quota rules.

Postgraduate admissions

MBA, M.Tech Machine Design and M.Tech Microwave & Communication Engineering are listed in the published fee schedule.

For current eligibility, seat matrix, application dates, counselling codes and management quota procedures, check the college admissions office and the latest official notification. Do not rely on old admission dates.
